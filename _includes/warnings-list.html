{%- comment -%} Renders the South African Weather Service warning mirror. The data comes from _data/saws_warnings.yml, which is written by .github/scripts/fetch_saws_warnings.py on a schedule - see the header of that file for why this cannot be a fetch() in the browser. Every card is stamped with data-expires so assets/js/warnings.js can re-check the expiry against the visitor's own clock. Between two runs of the workflow a warning can lapse, and a stale "in force" badge on a severe-weather page is worse than no badge at all.
