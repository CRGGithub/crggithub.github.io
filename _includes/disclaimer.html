This is a teaching and research test bed

The NWU Lekwena Radar and the NWU-WRF exist so that students can learn to build and break weather forecasting and now-casting systems. Either can be switched off for maintenance, or broken on purpose in the name of learning. Nothing published here may be used to issue severe-weather alerts — the South African Weather Service is the only institution mandated to do that. Treat these products as a tool for developing young scientists, and as something interesting to look at.
