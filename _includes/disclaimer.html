Teaching and research facility

The Lekwena radar and the NWU-WRF are operated for teaching and research. Either system may be taken offline for maintenance or modified in the course of student work, and its output is not quality-assured for operational use. Nothing published here may be used to issue severe-weather alerts: the South African Weather Service holds that mandate.
